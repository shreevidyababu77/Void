PRODUCT ASSUMPTIONS
1. Product Overview
Void is a free productivity and  screentime management platform that rewards users with points for completing productive activities and collaborate with friends 

2. Problem Statement
Students in today's digital age face an unusual challenge: "doomscrolling," the act of mindlessly consuming negative information and content online. This constant exposure to negativity harms students' mental health, causing anxiety, lack of sleep, triggering anger issues, and also stress. It also affects academic efforts, causing attention, procrastination, and concentration. Doom-scrolling distorts time management by converting minutes into hours wasted in an unending scrolling. 

3. Target Users
	Students constantly face this problem of increased screentime. This reduces their productivity, affects their academic performance and mental health. Also the people who want to reduce mindless scrolling.

4. Main Use Case
	Users set a study or productivity goal, complete a focus session, earn points, and use these points for a planned break.

5. Core Features
	Our platform has customizable goals, a focus timer, a points system, break redemption, progress tracking with a feature to connect with the friends.

6. User Assumptions
	Students want to reduce their screentime but find it difficult to control their scrolling habits. Completing a study session before break would excite the students to complete more study sessions for a break. Other users also will get motivated towards productivity


7. MVP Scope — Included
A dashboard displaying focus goals and earned points.
A form to create a study or productivity goal.
A working focus timer.
Points awarded when a focus session is completed.
A way to redeem points for a break.
Progress tracking using sample data.
A simple history of completed sessions and redeemed breaks.
Connect with friends and compete with them.

8. MVP Scope — Excluded
Real account registration and backend authentication.
Database integration or cloud synchronization.
Actual system-wide blocking of apps such as Instagram or YouTube.
Paid subscriptions, payments, or monetary penalties.
Advanced analytics and AI-powered recommendations.
Social features, leaderboards, or friend challenges.
